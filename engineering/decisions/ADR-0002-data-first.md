# ADR-0002: Data quality and point-in-time history precede alpha claims

**Status:** Accepted.

The first milestone is a permitted FYERS raw-data recorder and trustworthy
point-in-time research datasets. No strategy is considered validated until its
input data, universe, corporate actions, timestamps, costs and experiment search
are reproducible. Zerodha is a later independent execution integration; it is
not assumed to supply free market data under the Personal API tier.
