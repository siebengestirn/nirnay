# Predecessor source provenance

`extras/` is the promoted, tested reference subset of the predecessor
`engineering-challenge` source supplied as `engineering-challenge-main.zip`.
The full pre-cleanup tree — including the historical Kalshi/Polymarket
collector, experiments, non-India tests, schemas, measurements and operational
evidence — is preserved outside the runtime repository:

```text
../legacy-snapshot-2026-11-13/legacy-full-backup-2026-11-13.tar.gz
SHA-256: a49e864308a7e58d667a86049dd80e8ff2c631cd762e50a74aff60007464f9b1
```

The exact removed-file list is
`../legacy-snapshot-2026-11-13/legacy-removed-manifest-2026-11-13.txt`.

`extras/` is a separate Cargo workspace (`nirnay-extras`), not a member of the
Nirnay Cargo workspace. Its credentials, captured provider outputs and live
paths are not activated by V4.

The Upstox V3 protobuf decoder and schema were restored to active workspace
paths under `crates/upstox-feed-proto/` and `schemas/upstox/v3/`. The original
Upstox smoke client was restored to `apps/upstox-tools/`. The predecessor
README is kept as `README.upstream.archive` for provenance.
